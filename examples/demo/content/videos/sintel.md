## About this film

**Sintel** is the third open movie from the Blender Foundation, released in 2010. A lone young woman
named Sintel searches for Scales, the baby dragon she once rescued and raised. The film was produced
entirely with free and open-source software and released under Creative Commons Attribution.

## Why it's worth studying

- Early showcase of Blender's hair, cloth, and fluid simulation at feature-film scale
- Every production file is public, so you can open the actual shots and learn from them
- A compact example of three-act structure in under 15 minutes
